# Using FILTHY

On the worn Wrist-Boy, hold the right trigger and squeeze the right grip to cycle forward through **Stats**, **Items**, **Data**, and **FILTHY**.

In the paused, full-size Pip-Boy, point at **FILTHY** on the bottom tab row and pull the right trigger. You can also hold the right trigger and squeeze the right grip to move forward through the tabs, or the left grip to move backward.

On the paused Pip-Boy, FILTHY uses the open Data screen when entering from that tab. Moving back to Data restores the page that was open there.

FILTHY opens to **General settings**. Move the right stick up or down to browse. Press A to select a row, move the stick left or right to adjust a value, and press B to go back. On the full Pip-Boy, you can also point at a row and pull the right trigger.

Choose **Wrist flashlight** for beam settings, **Seated height** for calibration, or **Features** to choose which gameplay features are enabled. Lower the Wrist-Boy to return to play.

## Reload mode

**FILTHY → General → Reload mode** shows the active **Automatic** or **Manual** setting. Select it to switch modes and save your choice. The **Reload mode switching** feature still controls the optional controller shortcut. If you already enabled that feature in RC23, your saved mode carries over.

Keep your INIs and controller profiles when updating. Without a saved Filthy preference, General follows TTR’s reload setting.
