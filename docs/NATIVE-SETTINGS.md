# Using FILTHY

On the Wrist-Boy, hold the right trigger and squeeze a grip to cycle through the main tabs. On the full Pip-Boy, you can also point at FILTHY and pull the right trigger.

Once inside FILTHY, release the trigger. Use right grip for the next page or left grip for the previous page. Move the right stick up or down to browse and left or right to adjust a setting. A selects and B backs out. On the full Pip-Boy, you can point at rows and page labels. On the Wrist-Boy, lower your wrist to return to normal play.

## Equipment

Open Equipment, choose a wheel, profile and slot, then browse your carried items. The wheel highlights the destination slot and previews the selected item. A assigns the item. B backs out of the picker. **Save profile** keeps your assignments. **Use profile** activates the saved wheel. Use Holsters to save, preview and lock marker positions. Use Kits to combine a holster layout with both wheel profiles.

## Voice Commands

Voice uses TTR’s recognizer and your Windows default microphone. Press A to listen. Try “How many stimpaks do I have?” or “Turn on the flashlight.”
